Lubilanji River Rafting Experience

Lubilanji River Rafting Experience is an adventure tourism company offering rafting, river excursions, camping, and ecotourism activities on the Lubilanji River in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The company is dedicated to creating thrilling and safe outdoor experiences while promoting sustainable tourism and local culture.

History

Lubilanji River Experience was born from a vision inspired in 2004 when the founder arrived in Mbuji-Mayi to pursue his studies. During a student excursion to the Lubilanji River, he was deeply impressed by the beauty and adventure potential of the river, and the idea came to his mind to create something unique that could attract tourists and showcase the natural treasures of the region.

Over time, this vision grew into Lubilanji River Experience, an adventure and ecotourism company dedicated to providing safe, exciting, and authentic experiences while promoting nature, local culture, and community development. The company has become a symbol of adventure and ecotourism in the region, attracting tourists from around the world to discover the wonders of the Lubilanji River and its surroundings.
